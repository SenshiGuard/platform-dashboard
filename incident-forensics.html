Stage 04 • Active Data Exfiltration & C2
Magecart Credit Card Skimmer & Remote Drop Server Communication

What the Malware was Actively Doing (Attacker Intent):

The attacker hooked customer checkout submissions. When a buyer enters their payment details, the malware copies the credit card number, CVV, expiry, and billing address, and sends an asynchronous HTTP POST to remote Command & Control drop server http://194.26.112.44/c2/receive.php before allowing WooCommerce to finish checkout.
